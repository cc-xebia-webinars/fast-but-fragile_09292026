Add a loyalty points program to the shop.

Customers earn 1 point for every whole dollar of their order after the bulk discount and before shipping and tax. A $149.99 order earns 149 points. Points can be redeemed in blocks of 100, and each block is worth $5.00 off the next order. A redemption can't take more than 50% off the order's discounted goods, and it never applies to shipping or tax. Points expire 365 days after they're earned, and points earned on the 365th day before today are still valid. A customer can see their current balance and the date their oldest points expire.
